## v284 — REDO STEP 2 moves to 3; number pad 6 always moves right

- On Approve Artwork, REDO STEP 2 is now the 3 key (the number pad 3, or the 3 on the top row).

- The top-row 4 and 6 still pick versions 4 and 6, as before.
- The REDO STEP 2 key can still be changed on the Settings page. Its help text now says not to use 4 or 6.
