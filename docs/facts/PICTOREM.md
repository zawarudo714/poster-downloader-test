# Pictorem (pictorem.com) — what the shop offers a travel-poster seller

Read on 2026-11-05 from Pictorem's public pages in a signed-out browser, plus the
owner's two screenshots of the artwork editor (Cortina d Ampezzo Italy, uploaded
2026-11-05). `MEASURED` here means "Pictorem's own page said this on that date";
a promise on a page is not a tested result. Forum and blog reports are marked
as such. Nothing was signed into or changed.

## Verdict on buyers

- Pictorem does not bring buyers the way FineArtAmerica and TeePublic did. Its
  artist page says it "is in the printing business and not the gallery
  business", and step 2 of its four steps is "Share it" (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, `/artist.html`).
- The gallery ranks artworks by popularity, which "can be influenced by the
  amount of traffic that you bring" (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, `/faq.html`, "How can I become a featured artist?").
- The FAQ tells artists to "take more energy in promoting your art" while
  Pictorem handles the rest (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, `/faq.html`, "selling on Pictorem and selling on your Shopify Store").
- A public gallery does exist, with browsing by style, room, colour and themes
  such as Cityscapes and Vintage Posters (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, `/collection.html` menu).
  No page mentions paid ads, Google Shopping, Pinterest or email promotion of artists' work (`LEAD`: not found ≠ absent).
- Premium adds "Featured Artist Rotation — weekly visibility on our main
  collection page" (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, `/artistpremium.html`). How many buyers that brings is unknown (`LEAD`).
- Seller reports (forums, not Pictorem): one seller had 112 images and no sales
  after a year; another had 3 sales in about two years; a photographer who
  marketed his own Pictorem gallery had 5 sales in September 2024 (`LEAD`,
  microstockgroup.com thread "Pictorem.....Worthwhile?" via search summary;
  backyardsilver.com "Update on Fine Art Print Sales", 2024-09-24, read).
- Ahrefs estimated about 2.5K US visits a month from the search word
  "pictorem" (April 2025) (`LEAD`, third-party estimate via search).
- **House competitor:** Pictorem's own profile "VINTAGE POSTER" sells 34 pages
  of old travel posters (for example Berne Switzerland, Mexico City), 24×36
  satin poster at $53 (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, `/profile/VINTAGE.POSTER`, `/70305/berne-switzerland/`).

## The comparison: what FineArtAmerica and TeePublic say (owner asked for proof, 2026-11-05)

- TeePublic: "TeePublic invests in marketing the work of Artisans using
  dynamically generated ads across Google, Facebook, and other platforms", and
  features artists "in email, SMS, and onsite communications" (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`,
  teepublic.com/blog/how-marketing-is-handled-on-teepublic, dated 2024-04-09).
  The same post says artists who share their links earn 13× more.
- FineArtAmerica: "Our website is visited by millions of buyers each week"
  (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, fineartamerica.com/sell-art-online). No FAA page found
  that names paid ads; its listed tools are artist-run (social posts, discount
  codes, emails) (`LEAD` that FAA buys ads).
- The owner's FAA account: 70 sales, $1,477.21 gross (`MEASURED`, `docs/facts/FAA_BEHAVIOUR.md`).
  The owner never promoted the FAA shop (owner, 2026-11-05), so FAA itself
  brought all 70 buyers.
- Traffic estimates (third party, via search): Similarweb May 2026 about 3.1M
  visits for FAA and 8.6M for TeePublic; Ahrefs April 2025 about 63K search
  visits a month for Pictorem (`LEAD`, different tools and months).
- Pictorem has no page like TeePublic's: nothing says it pays to show artists'
  work, and its own words put promotion on the artist (above).

## Upload limits and files

- Free plan: 250 artwork uploads in total. Premium: unlimited (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, `/artistpremium.html`).
  No per-day limit is stated (`LEAD`).
- Premium costs $4.99 USD a month, billed $59.88 a year, with no automatic
  renewal (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, `/artistpremium.html`).
- Premium also adds: own domain, AI keywords, AI titles, promo codes up to
  15%, digital downloads, 5 custom pages, ZIP export of all images and listings,
  50 AI upscales a day (10 on free), faster preview generation (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, `/artistpremium.html`).
- Gallery files: "upload files with extension .jpg or .jpeg"; filenames
  alphanumeric only (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, `/faq.html`). Premium: "Files up to
  2GB, plus TIF, PSD and PNG" (`/artistpremium.html`). The free-plan size cap is not stated (`LEAD`).
- Best upload: original size without upscaling, highest-quality JPEG, sRGB (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, `/faq.html`).
- Print sizes per artwork: longest side 8–96 inches by default (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, owner's screenshot).
- Previews for all products can take up to 24 hours (made overnight) (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, `/faq.html`).
- Bulk upload: none found. The artwork list has "Select all" and "Bulk Edit"
  (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, owner's screenshot); a bulk upload tool is `LEAD`.

## Money

- The artist sets the amount; Pictorem adds its printing cost; "No commission
  on your sales", "No subscription or registration fees" (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, `/artist.html`).
- "% of Profit" is a percentage of the printing cost, not of the retail price.
  Pictorem's example: cost $200 + 50% = $300 retail, artist gets $100
  (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, `/faq.html`, "How do i calculate how much profit"). So 100% doubles the price:
  cost $40 → buyer pays $80 → artist gets $40.
- Exact base cost per size: the account's artwork admin, the EYE icon with a $
  (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, `/faq.html`).
- Payouts: press "Ask for payment" in Sales history; paid 30 days after the
  print ships; PayPal with no minimum; wire transfer minimum $500 with a $50 fee
  (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, `/faq.html`). The artist page also lists Interac (Canada)
  and bank transfer in 40+ countries (`/artist.html`). No PayPal fee is mentioned (`LEAD`).
- Pictorem says most artists use 75%–150% profit, or $2–5 per inch, or
  $10–25 per square foot (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, `/faq.html`, "At what price should I display my art?").
- Prices include free ground shipping in the continental US and Canada (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, `/faq.html`).
- The artist sees each buyer's name, address, phone and email (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, `/faq.html`).
- Public print prices, signed out, default options (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, `/order.html`):
  satin poster 24×16 $29, 36×24 $44; stretched canvas 24×16 $68, 36×24 $118.
  Whether the gallery's base cost equals these is `LEAD`: the house 24×36 poster
  sells at $53 against $44 here, and 16×24 at $37 against $29.

## Rules

- No exclusivity; leave whenever you want (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, `/artist.html`).
- The artist must own the rights; Pictorem "may... delete or remove Your
  Content at any time and for any reason" (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, `/terms.html`).
  The terms name a "Content Policy" that was not found (`LEAD`).
- No rule on AI-made art was found in the terms or FAQ. Pictorem itself sells
  an "AI Wall Art Generator" and gives Premium artists 50 AI images a day, so AI
  work is plainly welcome (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, `/artistpremium.html`, footer). A disclosure rule is `LEAD`.
- No rule on landmarks or brands was found (`LEAD`). The general rights rule
  above covers logos and trademarks.
- No approval step before a listing goes live was found; every print file is
  checked by a person before printing, with a possible $10–20 fix charge
  (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, `/faq.html`, "Are all the images reviewed prior printing?").
- Gallery products: fine art matte paper, poster, custom frame, canvas roll,
  stretched canvas, acrylic, brushed metal, HD metal, mural, wood, puzzle
  (`MEASURED 2026-11-05`, `/artist.html`). A Shopify app also exists (`/faq.html`).
