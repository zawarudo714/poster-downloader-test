DESIGNS PER ACCOUNT is a testing limit, 0 means check them all. CURES BEFORE FLAGGING is how many failed off-and-on cycles before a design is marked as a probably-vague tag and left alone. CONTINUE WITHIN decides how much CONTINUE remembers: at 24 it picks up a sweep interrupted last night, but a sweep you stopped three days ago would be started over. GIVE UP AFTER BLOCKED IN A ROW stops an account when TeePublic's full-page interstitial is in the way — those failures take three seconds each and tell us nothing about the designs, so the whole run waits half an hour and comes back instead of grinding through them. FLAG AFTER REAL FAILURES only counts failures on a page that was plainly TeePublic's, and only applies to switching OFF — a design we hid and cannot un-hide is retried every sweep for as long as it takes, because it earns nothing meanwhile.
